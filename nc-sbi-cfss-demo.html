Technical Architecture

CMS Platform

WordPress or Drupal — industry-standard open-source, fully managed hosting with automated patching and updates.

Identity Management

Microsoft Entra ID for internal NC SBI users. Separate MFA flows for external contributors and public users.

Four Secure Portals

Law enforcement, educators, students, and public — each with audience-appropriate auth, content, and branding.

Training LMS

SCORM-compatible learning modules, progress tracking, certificate generation, and CEU integration per portal.

CDN & Edge Security

Cloudflare Enterprise-tier CDN with DDoS mitigation, WAF, and global edge nodes ensuring sub-200ms load times.

Staff Training & Support

Dedicated onboarding for CFSS staff, admin documentation, help desk SLA, and ongoing content management support.
